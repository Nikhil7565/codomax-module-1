# Day 1 - Introduction to AI and Machine Learning

## 1. What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples:
- Chatbots
- Voice assistants
- Face recognition
- Recommendation systems
- Self-driving vehicles

## 2. What is Machine Learning (ML)?

Machine Learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence. It allows computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every situation.

Examples:
- Spam email detection
- Movie recommendations
- Fraud detection
- Predicting house prices

## 3. What is Deep Learning (DL)?

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.

Examples:
- Image recognition
- Speech recognition
- Natural language processing
- Autonomous vehicles

## 4. AI vs ML vs Deep Learning

| Technology | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| AI | Machines performing tasks that require intelligence | Chatbot |
| ML | Machines learning from data | Spam detection |
| Deep Learning | ML using multi-layer neural networks | Face recognition |

Relationship:

AI → Machine Learning → Deep Learning

## 5. Real-World Applications of AI

### Healthcare
AI can help analyze medical images and assist in disease detection.

### Education
AI can provide personalized learning and recommendations.

### Banking
AI can help detect fraudulent transactions.

### E-commerce
AI is used for product recommendations and customer support.

### Transportation
AI is used in navigation, traffic prediction, and autonomous driving technologies.

### Entertainment
AI is used to recommend movies, music, and other content.
